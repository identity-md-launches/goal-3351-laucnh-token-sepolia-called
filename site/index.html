How points work

  • One point per second is generated by the pool and split across all stakers in proportion to their active stake.
  • If nobody is staked, that period earns zero points for everyone.
  • Withdrawing some or all of your stake does not erase points you already earned; it only changes your share of points earned from that moment forward.
  • pointsOf(address) returns a value scaled by 1e18 per whole point, matching the token's base-unit convention.
